# 12. Quaderno Elettronico Theme

**Description**: Clean, modern corporate theme extracted from quadernoelettronico.it, featuring primary blue accents and soft neutral backgrounds, designed for healthcare software.

## Colors (Light Mode)

- **Primary Background**: `#F8FAFD` (Soft Cool Off-White)
- **Secondary Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Primary Text**: `#1E2022` (Dark Charcoal)
- **Secondary Text**: `#77838F` (Slate Gray)
- **Primary Accent**: `#377DFF` (Vivid Blue - Windent Brand)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#F80086` (Vivid Magenta - Poliwin Brand)

## Colors (Dark Mode)

- **Primary Background**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
- **Secondary Background**: `#1E293B` (Slate 800)
- **Primary Text**: `#F8FAFD` (Soft Cool Off-White)
- **Secondary Text**: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)
- **Primary Accent**: `#377DFF` (Vivid Blue)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#F80086` (Vivid Magenta)

## Fonts

- **Headers**: `Poppins`
- **Body**: `Poppins` (Fallback: `Arial, Helvetica`)
